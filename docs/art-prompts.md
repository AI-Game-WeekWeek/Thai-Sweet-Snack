# Image-generation prompts (watercolor, vivid, one style)

Every image below is a **separate piece** on a transparent or plain white background, so it can be cut out and dropped
into the game. Paste the **style block** at the start of every prompt so all pieces match.

## Style block (use for every piece)
> vibrant whimsical watercolor illustration, "crazy" saturated juicy colors, wet-on-wet color bleeds and splashes, soft
> paper grain, thin loose ink outline, glossy highlights, mouth-watering and cheerful, cute Thai street-dessert feel,
> single object centered, isolated on plain white background, no text, no shadow on the floor, square 1024x1024

Negative: photo-realistic, 3D render, dark or muddy colors, text, watermark, Chinese lanterns, red-and-gold Chinese style.

## 1. Finished desserts (served look, one image each; the game draws 3–8 copies)
1. **ข้าวต้มมัด** – one banana-leaf parcel tied with string, sticky rice peeking out of one end, banana and black bean visible in a cut half.
2. **ขนมชั้น** – one square slice of Thai layer cake, alternating white and pandan-green layers, glossy.
3. **ลูกชุบ** – one tiny mung-bean fruit (e.g. red chili or orange), glossy gelatin glaze, small green stem.
4. **ฝอยทอง** – one golden skein of egg-yolk threads folded into a bundle, syrup shine.
5. **ทองหยิบ** – one golden eight-petal pinched flower in a paper cup.
6. **ทองหยอด** – one round golden yolk ball in a paper cup, syrup shine.
7. **บัวลอย** – a small bowl of colored rice-flour balls (white, pink, green) in coconut milk.
8. **ขนมเบื้อง** – one folded crispy Thai crepe, white cream inside, golden threads on top.

## 2. Cooking scenes (one image per step; same order as the recipe, all objects separate)
Share the same table color. Draw props **without** background so the game can layer them.
- ข้าวต้มมัด: bowl of sticky rice with wooden spoon; black beans + banana slices; banana leaf laid flat with rice; leaf being rolled; tied parcel with string; steamer pot with parcels.
- ขนมชั้น: mixing bowl; two bowls (white / pandan green); steaming tray with growing layers; cooling tray; tray being cut with a knife.
- ลูกชุบ: pot of mung-bean paste; little paste balls on a board; fruit shapes with a paint brush; dipping in glaze; drying rack; box of finished fruits.
- ฝอยทอง: bowl of yolks with sieve; wok of boiling syrup; yolk threads drizzled in circles; wooden stick folding the skein; lifted skein draining; tray.
- ทองหยิบ: whisked yolks; small brass pan of syrup; yolk drop in syrup; pinching petals; paper cups.
- ทองหยอด: yolk dough on a board; syrup boiling in brass pan; little balls dropped into syrup; slotted spoon lifting; paper cups.
- บัวลอย: three colored doughs; rolling small balls; pot of boiling water with floating balls; ladling into bowl; pouring coconut milk.
- ขนมเบื้อง: batter bowl; whipped egg-white cream; batter spread on a round griddle; cream and golden threads; folding in half; tray.

## 3. Café (Thai, not Chinese)
- **Shopfront top:** Thai eave in teal and magenta with gold-trimmed pointed pennants, gold chofa (gable horn) corners.
- **Sign board** (blank, so the game writes "ร้านขนมไทย"): dark teak board with gold frame and chofa horns.
- **Jasmine garland (พวงมาลัย):** white jasmine buds with marigold, a red tassel, hanging vertically.
- **Teak wall** with carved gold lotus frieze (seamless horizontal strip), **terracotta floor tiles** (seamless).
- **Counter / table:** warm wood, seen from the front, empty top.
- **Menu board frame:** chalkboard in a teak frame, Thai pattern corners, empty inside.
- **Lucky cat (แมวกวัก)** waving, white with a red collar, two frames (arm up / arm down).
- **Old phone** (cream rotary phone), two frames (still / ringing).
- **Customers' heads:** round blank head slots (empty frames for profile pictures) with a Thai-garland rim, plus 6 cute body outfits in different colors.
- **VIP golden crown**, **order speech bubble** (empty), **coin** (gold, with a Thai-style stamp).

## 4. Effects (each on transparent background, 6–8 frames or one sprite sheet)
steam wisps; boiling bubbles; golden sparkles; heart (pink, filled and empty); heart burst; coin shower; tip-rain coins;
"yum" star burst when a customer is served; angry little cloud for a customer who leaves; lucky-cat glow; phone ring waves.

## 5. Gift menu icons
One small picture per tier (1, 5, 10, 20, 50, 99, 199, 500 coins): the dessert of that tier, 128x128, same style.
The board keeps an empty rounded 52x48 slot next to every name so these can be placed there.
